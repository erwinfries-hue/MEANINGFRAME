## Product principles that follow from these decisions

1. **Meaning before image.** The artwork must originate from the confirmed Meaning Map.
2. **Co-creation before automation theatre.** The customer’s decisions remain visible and consequential.
3. **Curated choice before unlimited choice.** Three art worlds, three paid variants, one guided revision.
4. **Preview before purchase.** The customer must experience a real personal result before paying.
5. **Premium before commodity.** The experience and presentation must justify the price.
6. **Honesty before mystique.** AI assistance is disclosed; fake artists, critics or human handiwork claims are prohibited.
7. **Privacy before growth tricks.** No public gallery, training reuse or indefinite storage.
8. **Real fulfilment before platform expansion.** The first physical orders are manually controlled and quality checked.
