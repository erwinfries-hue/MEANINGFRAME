# MEANINGFRAME — FOUNDER HANDOFF

## 1. Immediate brand actions

Register as soon as possible:

- `meaningframe.com`
- `meaningframe.ch`
- `meaningframe.de`

Recommended:

- use `meaningframe.com` as the product domain;
- redirect `.ch` and later `.de` to the correct local experience;
- keep domain and email administration at Hostpoint;
- connect the domain by DNS to Vercel, not by visible URL forwarding.

Create:

- `hello@meaningframe.com` or `info@meaningframe.com`
- `support@meaningframe.com`
- `privacy@meaningframe.com`

## 2. Accounts needed during the build

Do not create or pay for everything on day one. Claude Code Phase 0 must list the exact timing.

Expected:

- GitHub
- Vercel
- Supabase EU region
- Stripe
- Anthropic API
- Recraft API
- Resend
- Inngest

Optional after evaluation:

- PostHog EU
- Sentry

Never paste API keys into chat, product documents or Git. Configure them in local/hosting secret stores.

## 3. Founder information required before production

- legal entity name;
- legal address;
- commercial register/VAT information if applicable;
- legal, support and privacy contact emails;
- Swiss tax/VAT handling confirmed by accountant;
- refund/quality policy approved;
- payment payout account;
- support owner;
- fulfilment owner;
- Atelier Review owner, or feature disabled;
- approved print provider and sample;
- realistic production/delivery wording.

## 4. Print-sample decision

Before enabling Signature Edition:

1. generate one representative 4:5 Poetic Symbolism sample;
2. generate one Modern Essence vector sample;
3. order 40 × 50 cm framed samples in black and natural where feasible;
4. print the Curator’s Note;
5. evaluate in daylight and indoor light;
6. photograph packaging on arrival;
7. record actual total Swiss cost and delivery time;
8. approve or revise the Signature specification.

## 6. Approval method

After each phase, review:

- outcome;
- live/screenshots where relevant;
- requirements completed;
- tests;
- costs/vendors;
- unresolved risks;
- founder inputs.

Use the commands in `PHASE_COMMANDS.md` to approve the next phase.

Do not simply say “continue” after a failed gate. Use the failed-phase correction command.

## 7. Budget control

Target development frame:

- 8–10 weeks;
- CHF 7,500–10,000 ceiling;
- excludes substantial advertising and actual customer print/fulfilment cost.

Claude Code must maintain `COST_LEDGER.md`.

Any of the following requires explicit approval:

- paid vendor plan;
- material recurring subscription;
- additional market;
- additional product/material/size;
- additional AI provider in production;
- change to price;
- automated print-provider integration;
- work outside the current phase.

## 8. Recommended founder checkpoints

### After Phase 1

Review the brand and ten-second product comprehension.

### After Phase 2

Complete a Co-Author Session yourself and correct the Meaning Map language.

### After Phase 3

Review at least three example previews per Art World before accepting image quality.

### After Phase 4

Complete test purchases for both editions.

### After Phase 5

Approve a full digital delivery as if it were a real gift.

### After Phase 6

Physically inspect and sign off the print, frame, Curator’s Note and packaging.

### Before Phase 8

Confirm legal identity, policies, vendor settings, deletion proof and production support.

## 9. Initial beta recommendation

Recruit 8–12 Co-Authors with varied occasions:

- two milestone birthdays;
- two retirements/life-work recognitions;
- two long partnerships;
- two gratitude/parent/mentor projects;
- remaining cases across the three Art Worlds.

Use real consented inputs. Do not publish the results without a separate opt-in.
