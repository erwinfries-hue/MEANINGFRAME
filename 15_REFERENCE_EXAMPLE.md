# MEANINGFRAME — REFERENCE EXAMPLE

This is a fictional quality-calibration example. It must be labelled as fictional anywhere it appears publicly.

## 1. Project

Co-Author: Nina  
Subject: Elena  
Relationship: daughter and mother  
Occasion: retirement after a long teaching career  
Selected Art World: Poetic Symbolism  
Curator voice: Personal & Warm

## 2. Supplied source facts

- Elena taught primary school for 38 years.
- Nina describes her as warm, dependable, curious and quietly humorous.
- Elena kept handwritten notes from former pupils in a wooden box.
- The kitchen table was the place where family decisions and celebrations happened.
- Elena loves early walks by the lake.
- She brings postcards home from every journey.
- Nina remembers Elena saying, in her own words, that children need courage before they need perfect answers.
- Elena dislikes theatrical posing and bright neon colours.
- Desired recipient feeling: recognised, proud and ready for a new chapter.
- Preferred colours: warm blue, paper white, copper and muted green.

All later interpretation must remain grounded in these supplied facts.

## 3. Example Meaning Map

### Essence

Elena’s strength is quiet and sustaining. For almost four decades she helped children find confidence, while her warmth and dependability made the family kitchen table a place of orientation. Retirement is not presented as an ending, but as the opening of a more self-directed chapter shaped by curiosity, travel and the calm of the lake.

### What defines her

1. **Quiet encouragement**  
   Her work as a teacher and Nina’s remembered sentence connect courage with learning.

2. **Dependability**  
   Nina describes Elena as someone who was consistently present for pupils and family.

3. **Curiosity**  
   Travel postcards and the wish for a new chapter express ongoing openness.

4. **Warm, understated humour**  
   This should be felt through gentle lightness in the composition, not through comic imagery.

### Life anchors

- 38 years in the classroom
- handwritten pupil notes in the wooden box
- the family kitchen table
- early walks by the lake
- travel postcards

### Proposed symbols

- **An open window:** a new chapter and continued curiosity
- **Small paper forms becoming birds:** pupils’ handwritten notes becoming confidence and movement
- **A warm horizontal table line:** family steadiness and shared decisions
- **A lake horizon:** calm, reflection and space ahead

The Co-Author must confirm or remove each proposed symbol.

### Must include

- Elena remains recognisable
- calm forward-looking atmosphere
- subtle references to teaching and family
- a sense of openness rather than farewell

### Must avoid

- classroom cliché with blackboard and apple
- theatrical retirement celebration
- neon colour
- exaggerated smile or posed studio portrait
- visible text generated inside the artwork

## 4. Example Art Brief

### Identity

Elena is the recognisable central subject, represented with a calm natural expression and a dignified, unposed presence. Approved photo references define visible features; no age, personality or biography is inferred from appearance.

### Emotional core

Quiet contribution becoming personal freedom. The work should hold gratitude and pride while looking toward an open future.

### Composition

Elena is placed slightly left of centre. A warm table-like horizontal plane anchors the lower composition. Behind and around her, a restrained sequence of small paper forms gradually becomes abstract bird-like shapes moving toward an open, light-filled space. A distant lake horizon provides calm depth. The focal point remains Elena’s face and presence.

### Colour and light

Warm paper white and muted lake blue dominate. Copper light connects the subject to the table plane; muted green gives the open area a natural sense of renewal. Soft directional morning light, restrained contrast and painterly texture.

### Quality and exclusions

Premium contemporary symbolic painting; calm visual hierarchy; tactile but controlled texture; recognisable artistic likeness. No classroom props, readable text, generated signature, cartoon form, neon, spectacle or imitation of a named artist.

## 5. Example Curator’s Note — Personal & Warm

### Title

**The Open Chapter**

### Opening

This artwork honours the kind of influence that rarely needs to announce itself. Elena stands at its centre with the same quiet steadiness that shaped a family and accompanied generations of children through their first discoveries.

### Artwork reading

The composition is held together by a warm horizontal line. It recalls the kitchen table where conversations, decisions and celebrations found their place, but it also gives the entire picture a sense of reliability. Elena does not appear in a theatrical retirement scene. She is shown calmly and recognisably, with her attention turned toward the light that opens beyond her.

The small paper forms beginning near her refer to the handwritten notes she kept from former pupils. As they move through the image, their shapes become lighter and more open. They are not intended as literal birds, but as a visual expression of confidence passed on: something received in a classroom and carried independently into the world.

The lake horizon introduces distance without separation. It connects Elena’s early walks with the space that now lies ahead of her. Warm blue and muted green create calm, while copper light preserves the feeling of human closeness. Retirement therefore appears neither as a farewell nor as an empty pause. It becomes an open chapter shaped by the curiosity that has always been part of her.

### Symbols

**The paper forms**  
They begin with the pupil notes Elena kept and gradually suggest movement. Their meaning is the courage and confidence that can continue long after a lesson has ended.

**The table line**  
This stable element recalls the family kitchen table. It represents dependability, orientation and the many important moments that did not require a formal setting.

**The open light**  
The brighter space is deliberately not filled with a predetermined destination. It leaves room for journeys, postcards and choices that belong entirely to Elena’s next chapter.

**The lake horizon**  
Its quiet distance reflects Elena’s early walks and brings stillness to the image without making it static.

### Closing

The work says thank you without turning Elena’s contribution into a monument. It recognises what she has given, keeps her warmth at the centre and leaves the future visibly open.

### Co-creation disclosure

Co-created with AI from the memories, choices and confirmed Meaning Map provided by the Co-Author.

## 6. What this example demonstrates

- specificity comes from evidence, not exaggerated praise;
- proposed symbolism is confirmed before use;
- the subject remains the focal point;
- Curator’s Note explains real visual decisions;
- retirement is framed as a new chapter;
- AI assistance is disclosed without displacing the Co-Author.

## 7. Alternate Art World translation

### Modern Essence

Use a recognisable vector silhouette/profile, a stable copper table line, a sequence of paper shapes transforming into open geometric forms, and a restrained lake-blue horizon. Keep negative space and clean paths. Do not use clip-art birds.

### Narrative Collage

Use Elena as the central recognisable figure with a controlled editorial layer of the wooden note box, fragments suggesting the family table, a lake path and abstract postcard edges. Limit the narrative to four anchors and maintain one coherent light source.
