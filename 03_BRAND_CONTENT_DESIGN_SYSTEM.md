# MEANINGFRAME — BRAND, CONTENT & DESIGN SYSTEM

## 1. Brand core

Name: **MEANINGFRAME**  
Claim: **WHAT MATTERS, BECOMES ART.**  
Endorsement: **by AXIA4 Digital**

German explanatory line:

> Was einen Menschen ausmacht, wird zu Kunst.

English explanatory line:

> Personal art, co-created from what makes someone meaningful.

Campaign line:

> Honour someone who truly matters.

## 2. Brand idea

The name has three layers:

- **Meaning:** identity, memory, values and relationship
- **Frame:** artistic framing, interpretation and the finished object
- **MeaningFrame:** a process that gives personal meaning a visible form

## 3. Personality

MEANINGFRAME is:

- warm;
- dignified;
- contemporary;
- quietly confident;
- attentive;
- artistic but understandable;
- transparent about AI;
- premium without elitism.

MEANINGFRAME is not:

- loud;
- mystical without substance;
- technical;
- sentimental to the point of cliché;
- ironic or novelty-driven;
- falsely presented as human-painted.

## 4. Voice

Write with calm, clear, emotionally intelligent language.

Preferred:

> Tell us what makes this person unmistakably themselves.

Avoid:

> Feed our revolutionary AI everything about your loved one.

Preferred:

> Your Meaning Map is ready. Please check whether we understood what matters.

Avoid:

> Our AI has analysed the personality.

## 6. Visual direction — Contemporary Gallery

### Colour tokens

| Token | Suggested value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | `#F4F0E8` | Main background |
| Warm White | `#FBF9F4` | Cards and modal surfaces |
| Charcoal | `#252321` | Primary text |
| Stone | `#716B63` | Secondary text |
| Oxide | `#9B4A3C` | Primary accent and selected states |
| Copper | `#B67855` | Restrained secondary accent |
| Sage | `#778174` | Success and calm confirmation |
| Line | `#D8D0C5` | Borders and dividers |
| Error | `#A33B36` | Error state only |

All colour combinations must pass WCAG 2.2 AA for normal text.

### Typography

- Editorial display serif: a legally embeddable variable font such as Cormorant Garamond, Fraunces or a verified equivalent.
- UI sans: Inter, Geist or a verified equivalent.
- Do not use a thin serif for body text.
- Minimum body size: 16 px.

Claude Code must verify current font licences and performance before pinning a choice.

### Layout

- max content width approximately 1200 px;
- reading width 680–760 px;
- generous vertical rhythm;
- artwork shown large, never buried in UI chrome;
- one primary action per view;
- no gradients that compete with artwork;
- no animated particles, sparkles or “AI magic” clichés;
- motion respects reduced-motion preferences.

## 7. Logo direction

The MVP may use a refined wordmark plus a simple signet.

Signet concept:

- two offset frame lines converge around a small central open space;
- the open space represents meaning supplied by the Co-Author;
- avoid initials `MF` as the sole idea;
- avoid paintbrushes, palettes, robot stars and generic image icons.

The initial implementation must use an SVG asset, not rasterised logo text. It must work in:

- full wordmark;
- signet only;
- dark on Paper;
- Warm White on Charcoal;
- 32 px favicon.

Do not claim a final trademark-ready logo without visual review.
